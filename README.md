## About me

I'm **Christopher**, also known online as **Kissadevre**.

I'm a software and systems enthusiast from Mexico focused on **backend
development, Linux infrastructure, networking and open-source software**.

My main development stack is **PHP, Laravel and MariaDB/MySQL**, while much
of my systems work revolves around Linux, Docker, Nginx, Proxmox,
virtualization, networking and self-hosted infrastructure.

I like building software around **real problems rather than feature
checklists**. I prefer simple and maintainable systems, security considered
from the beginning, and technology that has a clear reason to exist.

Open source played an important role in how I learned software development,
so I try to give something back by making many of my own projects available
for others to use, study and modify.

I'm also the founder of **[Zibuu](https://zibuu.net/)**, where I combine
software, infrastructure and open source with a more colorful side of
technology — gaming, anime, furry culture and a bit of chaos. 🐾
